# Mexico: source-linked review after the October 2026 audit

The current working release corrects targeted references in the federal corpus. It does not establish a confirmed deficit count or a new mean legal-anchoring score. Source checks and unresolved legal judgments are recorded separately.

## Notification and surveillance

The SINAVE mandate is in General Health Law Article 133(II), not the disease list in Article 134. Final NOM-017 includes duties across technical-administrative levels (6.6.3), an express procedural referral (6.6.4), and immediate domestic notification to DGE (8.1.4). The referred international surveillance manual still needs examination. A missing document in a review is not proof of a missing procedure.

## Charges and health declarations

The international health regulation has relevant provisions on gratuity (Article 7), aircraft declarations (Article 30), and maritime declarations (Article 31). Prior absence claims are withdrawn. Scope, exceptions and current IHR-form alignment remain review questions.

## National IHR Authority

PAHO reported Mexico’s designation or establishment of an Authority by 15 July 2026 (CD63/INF/1, paragraph 17). That report does not name the Mexican entity or reproduce the domestic designation act. The dataset must not infer DGE=NIA from DGE’s separate focal-point role.

## Use and limitations

A legal recommendation needs the specific obligation, relevant provisions, competent authorities and applicable legislative or administrative route. No universal need for a new statute is inferred. The network represents documentary associations, not observed cooperation or political power.
