# Review and capacity-building entry points

This is a workflow for examining legal questions with competent authorities, not a list of confirmed legislative defects.

## Start with the obligation and the source

Locate the relevant provisions, follow their references and identify their applicable versions. A reference to an unreviewed manual should be recorded as a research task, not as an absent procedure. Compare all relevant sources before deciding whether the obligation is sufficiently specified.

## Identify the nature of the decision

Distinguish an authority's mandate, a reporting procedure, a coordination agreement, a resource question and a limit of documentary coverage. A regulation is not inherently inadequate and a new law is not the default remedy. The need for legislative, regulatory, budgetary or oversight action requires examination of the particular competence and subject matter.

The [WHO–IPU handbook](https://www.ipu.org/file/14338/download) discusses law-making, oversight and budgetary allocation. These functions can guide questions but do not predetermine which institution must act on every mapping record.

## Examine the new Authority's mandate

PAHO reports Mexico's designation or establishment of a National IHR Authority. Retrieve the domestic instrument and identify how it interacts with focal-point, health regulatory, border and subnational functions. The public report alone does not settle the entity's identity or legal powers.

## Keep legal relations and practice separate

The Actors Explorer includes four individually sourced legal-relation records. They document duties in legal texts, not successful information exchanges. Administrative records, interviews, exercises or other operational evidence would be needed to investigate performance.

## Validation before use

Review candidate matches, rights protections, legal references and the remaining annotations with qualified domestic experts before using this pilot for policy or advocacy. No revised gap count or aggregate score has been certified. The [audit record](/audit) identifies what changed, what is retained as historical coding, and what is still pending.
