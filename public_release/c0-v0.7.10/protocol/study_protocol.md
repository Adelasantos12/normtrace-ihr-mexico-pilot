# Study protocol

## Research question

Can a provision-level protocol produce stable classifications of formal legal specification under a prespecified model condition?

## Design

The study evaluates classification stability in a frozen population of 1,117 obligation-provision pairs. A stratified main sample contains 400 pairs. A disjoint pilot sample contains 280 pairs. The registered model condition uses the OpenAI Responses API with GPT-5.6 Sol, high reasoning effort, isolated context, no prior messages, and no automatic retries.

## Claim boundary

The experiment measures computational stability of the coding protocol. It does not validate Mexican legal accuracy, retrieval completeness, implementation, compliance, effectiveness, or equivalence with another model or interface.
